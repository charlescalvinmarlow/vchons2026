Project Synopsis

Tracing roots to a plantly attention is a workshop kit that includes a series of exploratory mapping and drawing exercises, designed to encourage Filipino Australians to reflect on their relationships to culture and plants.

While working in the PAACI (Philippine Australian Arts, Culture and Innovation Centre) committee, I began thinking about how we can better attune ourselves to the non-human world, especially in the design of PAACI programs. This led me to explore ways I could embed plants of cultural significance, such as calamansi and sampaguita, in participatory activities to strengthen our Filipino Australian communities.

The workshop ‘Tanim’ aims to cultivate a Filipino experience of 'ginhawa' — or ease and synergy of human and nature. The workshop collateral was developed through a co-creation image making practice with plants, notably through mark-making and taking inspiration from botanical forms and colour.

Guided through the stages of growth from ground to garden, the workshop prompt cards invite participants to place themselves in a reflective state and put these thoughts into tangible form through drawing and mapping focused action cards.

In considering the embodiment of a plant from seed, stem and blooming into a flower, participants will leave with a deeper, shining attention to the plants they encounter and the ecologies they exist within.
