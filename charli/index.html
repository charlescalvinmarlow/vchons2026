Charli Krite

Objects of Interest

Charli is a designer living, thinking and making on Gadigal Land, with a focus in print and publication design. Her designs are driven by practice-based research and thinking with the other-than-human.

Project Synopsis

Objects of Interest explores how everyday objects can help make sense of the world around us. The project began with a series of design experiments and writing to better understand Jane Bennett's theory of object agency; my research expanded to also consider ideas from sociologists and philosophers including such as Bruno Latour, Jean Baudrillard and Bill Brown. My practice-based research, which included observational drawing and participatory workshop activities, centered on objects from my personal life — their lives within collections, their own relationships and their fate within a world of increasingly ephemeral things. The publication has three chapters, each including one research essay, a series of illustrations and reflections on objects in my life. The publication reflects how my personal objects act as anchors, grounding me in a time where the commodification of things often outweighs their personal value.
