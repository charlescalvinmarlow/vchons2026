Cheylon Devine is a multidisciplinary designer passionate about creating things that sprinkle little moments of delight through our everyday lives. A creative generalist, she employs creative problem solving in a wide range of formats from digital to print design, and has a soft spot for illustration and animation. Her love of the ocean, snorkelling and diving inspires her creative practice.

Project Synopsis

VESTIGES OF THE SEA is a collection of three display typefaces inspired by marine creatures, with accompanying type specimens that showcase the construction of the letterforms and also educate visitors about the creatures that inspired them. The project explores the potential for type specimens to serve as a medium of communication between designers, a platform for raising important issues in addition to promoting typefaces.

The three typefaces are: Echinoidea, inspired by sea urchins, Anthozoa, based on hard corals, and Gastropoda, taking influence from the shells of sea snails. All three of these creatures leave behind calcium carbonate skeletons when they die, which are then washed up onto the sand, the only traces of ocean life that most people can freely interact with. Vestiges of the Sea thus invites people to consider the living creatures beyond the hollow vestiges we see on the beach, and celebrate the wonderful creatures that make the ocean their home.

The display typefaces are intended to draw designers in to explore the type specimens, where educational information about sea creatures is woven through visuals and motion graphics showcasing the letterforms’ construction. Designers interacting with the type specimens thus have an opportunity to learn something about marine life that they didn’t expect to learn.

It is now more important than ever to learn about, and care about, the ocean and its inhabitants. Despite the fact that the ocean sustains all life on earth, it is so far removed from many of our daily lives that it is easy to never think about it. This oversight has allowed the ocean to be continuously exploited, so that it now exists in a precarious ecological state. By educating viewers about sea creatures, Vestiges of the Sea aims to foster a stronger ethics of care for the ocean.
