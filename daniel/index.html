Project Synopsis

Everything Goes is a web portfolio of critical writings on graphic design. It’s intended to allow me to bring back writing as a core part of my creative practice, and will be updated periodically. The articles cover a range of topics, from remix theory to the hipster subculture, but share a focus on graphic design in relation to music, history and pop culture.

I was drawn to writing as I felt unable to express my ideas adequately through my design work alone. Moreover, I thought that my perspective, as a designer coming into industry in the midst of the generative AI boom, was worth sharing—and could be of value to others in a similar position.
