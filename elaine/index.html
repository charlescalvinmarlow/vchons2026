Elaine Cabansag is a Sydney/Eora-based designer who believes every piece of visual media tells a unique story. As a print-based designer focused on human connection and interaction, she uses physical mediums and social media as a storytelling outlet.

Project Synopsis

PLAYGROUND is an interactive publication exploring play as a creative methodology. In an increasingly homogenised visual culture, shaped by algorithms and an emphasis on constant productivity, the project asks: how might emerging designers use playful prompts and activities cultivate their creative identity and resist pressure to produce work that is ‘on trend’?

Through a collection of user-tested prompts and activities, PLAYGROUND introduces different approaches to play through making, inviting users to engage with their surroundings and others. The publication is split into four sections: Play as Curiosity – wander and find inspiration in the everyday; Play as Connection – find joy in collaboration; Play as Rebellion – break convention and embrace mistakes; Play as Change – shake up familiar processes and discover new ways of creating.

PLAYGROUND frames play as a serious method, encouraging designers to enjoy the process of creative discovery over predetermined outcomes.
