Ellie Jiyoon Park

The Words He Left

Ellie Jiyoon Park (She/Her) is a Korean-Australian graphic designer and illustrator working on Gadigal Land in Sydney, Australia. She is passionate about publication design, illustration, typography and branding. Storytelling and designing with empathy are at the core of her practice. She likes to create thoughtful visual outcomes that balance emotion, meaning and clarity.

Project Synopsis

The Words He Left explores intergenerational language loss across three generations of my family, examining the relationship between language, migration, cultural heritage and personal identity. My grandfather's journey from North Korea to South Korea, followed by my mother's migration from South Korea to Australia, represents generations of movement across borders, languages and cultures. My family's history is shaped by displacement, migration and the search for belonging, yet much of my family history remains unspoken. Stories of the past aÍre difficult to revisit, leaving gaps in my understanding of where we came from and the experiences that shaped my family. Through family photographs, poetry, and conversations, The Words He Left makes visible experiences of linguistic fragmentation and cultural disconnection by filling the ‘gaps’ and ‘silence’ in language: the words my grandfather carried, the words my mother translated, and the words I am still learning how to carry forward…
