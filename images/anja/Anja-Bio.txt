Anja Clausius is a designer studying and working in Sydney, Australia. Interested in the possibilities of typography, print, motion, web, and video design, she seeks to explore and evolve forms of communication through both digital and physical means. Thinking critically and deliberately about how her work is translated into the world is imperative to her practice. Much of her process is informed by her love for knitting, cinema, and the natural landscape.
