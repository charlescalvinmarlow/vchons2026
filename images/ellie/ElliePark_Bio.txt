Bio


Ellie Jiyoon Park (She/Her) is a Korean-Australian graphic designer and illustrator working on Gadigal Land in Sydney, Australia. She is passionate about publication design, illustration, typography and branding. Storytelling and designing with empathy are 
at the core of her practice. She likes to create thoughtful visual outcomes that balance emotion, meaning and clarity.
