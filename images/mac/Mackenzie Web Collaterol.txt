Life is fleeting and can be unfair. It is vital to value who or what you have in your life while they are still present before the memories become objectified. ‘Here and Gone’ is an investigative visualisation of human sentimentalities and the objectification of memories. The project is led by a participatory workshop-based booklet developed to trigger episodic recollection. Supported by academic research, ‘Knick Knacks are Autobiographical’ and a data developed narrative publication, ‘Episodic Artifacts’ that contains evidence of the workshops ability to facilitate interactions and stimulate thought or emotion from participants to reflect on their own human connections that may be connected to their valued objects.BIO (25 words) Mackenzie Joyner aims to explore creatively innovative methods across digital and print formats to make audiences ponder. Central Coast based and practicing across Eora Nation and Darkinjung Country, she thrives with mixed media, data synthesization and visual storytelling. COLOURS Text: #0039ab (navyish blue)Back: #fff4dc (yellow tinged cream)Image Sequence: HERO: HERO_VID_WEB_Mackenzie_HereandGone.mp4 (.h264)1_img_WEB_Mackenzie_HereandGone.png2_img_WEB_Mackenzie_HereandGone.png3_img_WEB_Mackenzie_HereandGone.png4_img_WEB_Mackenzie_HereandGone.png[NOTE: I was having trouble converting to webp, if needed please do so to pngs. tysm]
