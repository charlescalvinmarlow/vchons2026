Project Synopsis

The Barrier is a year-long exploration into acknowledging artifice through (an)architectural methods of unbuilding, rebuilding and temporary autonomous zones. The portfolio of ex-periments explores ways these methods can build closer connections between maker and audience; seeking shared truths in the opaque walls of communication.

‘Type’ correlates temporary autonomous zones to the design of letterforms, through Sydney Park's smokestacks. This experiment explores how the walls of meaning can be explored as a literal space that includes and excludes entry to those who have a connection to place.

‘Screen’ centres on the Moiré, an ancient visual phenomenon that has become unsightly as a leftover of digital remediation. It rebuilds this unsightly excess, distilling it into a visual identity across a poster and invitation to a fictional party.

‘Skin’ attempts to unbuild the space between interior and exterior, removing the traditional barrier of representation, flattening the space between subject and outcome via material manipulation.

‘Fiction’ places these experiments into a meta-research project, asking whether material truth can exist within a lie. Rewriting my research paper and placing it into a dialogue be-tween myself and the designer of these works, I aim to uncover truth (and write a thesis).
