Project Synopsis

Typology of Oscillation is a two-part project investigating oscillation as a methodology for creative practice, through (1) a design research publication and (2) an ongoing monthly mail club. After noticing how 2020s visual culture reprises aesthetics of the past, I considered the way in which oscillation – the tendency to swing between oppositional cultural poles – can be a creative framework to analyse, predict and create emergent visual culture.

A return towards the past can be either radical or regressive: as a case study of how I could incorporate oscillation into a meaningful creative practice, I created Miniquest Club, a paid physical mail subscription exploring the return to snail mail as an example of resurgence towards analogue design.
