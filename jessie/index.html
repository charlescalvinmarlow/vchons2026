Project Synopsis

The Tales of Hoffmann is a speculative game based on the 20th Century opéra fantastique by Jacques Offenbach. In my game, players take the perspective of Hoffmann but here, his romance is set in the internet landscape of the early 2000s. 20th century opera and Y2K inspired interactive media aren’t usually paired, but I wanted to explore telling The Tales of Hoffmann in a new, experimental way that would appeal younger audience who aren’t commonly interested in opera.

Underneath its whimsical exterior, the original story cautions around love and machines, and wildly it rings true today. I translated the opera’s whimsy through logic-defying UI moments and sound design. Creating the musical arrangements allowed me to incorporate my skills as a musician within my design practice and explore a way of sharing my music that is removed from Spotify/Apple Music/YouTube Music. The project is a bid to reframe music as something contemporary audiences are allowed to participate in, linger on, and find meaningful.
