Project Synopsis

After something was sighted at an abandoned coastal Care Centre, a government task force was sent to investigate. The team went silent. You are part of a second team sent in to find out what happened.

This 15-minute VR horror prologue is played in a Meta Quest headset. Moving with your squad, armed with a rifle-mounted light, you only see what the beam reveals. Inspired by the slow, claustrophobic openings of Resident Evil and cinematic scale of Pacific Rim, the prologue treats the building as the storyteller. Rooms, stains, abandoned equipment and sudden flashes of lightning hint at what happened here before.

I created the game working with a team of AI tools for image generation, 3D models, code, sound and animation. The project asks: when AI can do the technical work, what is left for the designer? My answer: deciding what the experience should feel like, and checking that it does.

Put on the headset, switch on your light, and step inside.
