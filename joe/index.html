Project Synopsis

'Budots Is Serious Dance Music' is an experimental mixed media video essay, exploring and questioning the Filipino electronic dance genre, Budots and how it's being translated into Sydney's dance floors. The video's treatment aims to reflect the sampled and remixed nature of Budots as well as the everyday grit and expressive nature of Filipino street life and culture.

In today’s music and dance landscape, non-Western dance genres such as West Africa’s ‘Amapiano’, Brazil’s ‘Baile Funk’ and the Philippines’ ‘Budots’, is having its own moment in Western music festivals, events and parties. These global dance genres are being presented to a crowd unaware of its history, culture and meaning, diluting it to texture and novelty for a DJ set. As carriers of the sound, there’s a sense of responsibility for maintaining the context and meaning for unfamiliar audiences. Through an interview with Sydney-based Filipino DJ, Cutedeary and critical analysis on Budots writings by music writers, Jorge Juan B Wieneke, Sai Versailles and Sean Bautista, I undertook a year of research into how Budots came to be, the issues faced within the genre and the importance of respect and care for the sound. I navigated through the complexities of understanding and respecting the genre, while also translating it outside of its context. By remaining flexible and relational in my showcase of Budots, I implore those to critique my project and its insights to generate meaningful and purposeful conversations.
