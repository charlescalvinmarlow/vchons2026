Project Synopsis

CROAK is a short, three-part mixed-media animated story about a Frog’s journey through the digital afterlife. It brings a playful tone to existentialism and the impermanence of death. Through remediation—refashioning old media into new media—CROAK blends analogue and digital design methodologies.

Drawing on excerpts from Dante Alighieri’s Divine Comedy, the animation is structured into: Death, Limbo, and Rebirth. Frog experiences a surreal and vibrant journey in the Afterlife, emphasising that death is not the end. Frog’s journey through these spaces parallels the life cycles of analogue processes used to create the work: from analogue animation and light art to wall projections, and digital compositing and treatment. By shifting materiality between physical and digital spaces, new layers of remediation are added, giving analogue materiality new ‘afterlives’.

In a world where creative output is increasingly automated, sped up and digitised, and designers’ processes risk being overshadowed, remediation helps analogue materiality find a new place in a digital creative practice.
