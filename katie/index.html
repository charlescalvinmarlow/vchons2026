Katie Bayne

Healing the Internet, Bit by Bit

Katie Bayne is a local pixel artist and designer obsessed with grids, rainbows, and all things cute. Interested in translating between digital and tangible forms, she works with an interdisciplinary practice across accessible web, printed, and textile design.

Project Synopsis

How might a design practice rooted in Buddhist values of mindfulness and impermanence offer a more optimistic digital future?

Through hand-weaving a mandala, Healing the Internet, Bit by Bit explores how Buddhist frameworks of mindfulness and impermanence can inspire a more intentional and hopeful internet.
