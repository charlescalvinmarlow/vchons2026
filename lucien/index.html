Project Synopsis

With Me, Through The Tailwind is a two-part design fiction exploring media obsolescence and nostalgia through design practice. The two components - a collection of experiments and an archival publication - document a personal response to a possible loss of craft and material practice within tech-centric turn in Design. The fictive personas are an extension of my own conflicted feelings about the chasm between craft and commercial practices. The two fictions interact to present a tension for other emerging designers to consider.

The collection of experiments explore analogue and craft design processes that suggest a belonging to the past. Here, I focus on nostalgic aesthetics that can be achieved through the use of a typewriter, craft paper and CD/VHS covers; the experiments are portals to past eras and distant memories. Collectively, the experiments invite reflection on the mortality/obsolescence that all things (regardless of era) are eventually subject to.

The archival publication is a digital remediation of the experiments. By collating the experiments into a structured index, they ascend their physical form and encourage the reader to acknowledge their preservation in a reproducable book format, which can also be archived digitally as a PDF. A certain tactility is lost, however, drawing attention to what is lost in the remediation of hand-generated work into digital formats.

This project involved a slow, considered design process that centred on thinking-through-making. Fortunately, the perspectives and reflections developed throughout this process will now inform my future practice.
