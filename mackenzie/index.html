Project Synopsis

Memories are personal collections of past events or connections, so by collecting objects to represent them, it becomes easier to relive the moments. Life is fleeting and can be unfair. It is vital to value who or what you have in your life while they are still present before the memories become objectified.

‘Here and Gone’ is an investigative visualisation of human sentimentalities and the objectification of memories. The project is led by a participatory workshop-based booklet developed to trigger episodic recollection. Supported by academic research, ‘Knick Knacks are Autobiographical’ and a data developed narrative publication, ‘Episodic Artifacts’ that contains evidence of the workshops ability to facilitate interactions and stimulate thought or emotion from participants to reflect on their own human connections that may be connected to their valued objects.
