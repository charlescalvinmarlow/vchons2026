Project Synopsis

PersonaOS is an interactive, post-apocalyptic design fiction that critiques the increased, yet mismanaged autonomy and anthropomorphist features designed into current Large Language Models. In PersonaOS, you take the role of a scavenger traversing a post-apocalyptic world until stumbling across a working laptop. You must navigate the 'living' operating system installed on the device, controlled by a nefarious, dangerously intelligent digital assistant known as The Oracle. By interacting with and exploring the system and its features, whilst ensuring to regulate the Oracle’s mood meters, you unearth a dark secret: the fate of the previous owner and humanity itself.

Extrapolating current design trends within the LLM development space, PersonaOS presents a frightening yet poignant fiction that speaks directly to the ways this technology is currently being poorly managed, hopefully leaving users with a healthy skepticism towards using LLM technology.

PersonaOS comprises of two parts: a webpage, accompanied by a series of publications designed to further communicate the design fiction, help use the site, and documentation of my research and design processes.
