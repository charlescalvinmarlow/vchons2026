Project Synopsis

'Menagerie’ is a drawing game where you explore the city, collecting stray memories from another universe. The game is designed for people who love to create and want to interact with the city in a new way. This project aims to foster new ways to experience place, turning the city into a playground for art, storytelling, and exploration.

In this world, forgotten memories take the form of beasts and roam the earth. As a new recruit, you will assist by recapturing memories that have escaped Menagerie. Scour the records to gain insight before heading into the real world to capture memories by designing a creature based on what you’ve learnt.

Inspired by the genre of solo journaling role-playing games, each person’s experience of Menagerie will be different. Enjoy each aspect of this game at your own pace to build your unique Menagerie of memories.
