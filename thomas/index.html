Tom Houston is a multidisciplinary designer and photographer, practicing on Gadigal land. A devout generalist, his practice often intersects photo-media, motion and typography. His work is driven by an infectious curiosity and a love for the eclectic corners of culture and history.

Project Synopsis

What would an age-old fable about the planet Earth and its place in the wider universe look like?

'Are You My Twin' is an experimental short film, following the planet Earth on its search across the universe for its ‘twin planet’ - Since one habitable planet isn’t enough for ‘the humans’.

The output of my project is a proof-of-concept animation, developing a visual language for this unique cosmic fable. This project delivers to a live brief with filmmaker Ceridwen Dovey and the Archival Futures Collective.
